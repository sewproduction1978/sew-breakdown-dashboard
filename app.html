A production entry only counts toward the next station's WIP if its department and process match a stage in that part's current routing exactly. If a part's route changed after entries were logged against the old stage name, those entries get stranded here — invisible everywhere else. People & Access

  1. Add the person here with their role.
  2. In Supabase → Authentication → Users → Add user → Create new user, type the same email and a password, tick Auto Confirm User.
  3. Give them the password. They open the app and sign in — they can change the password from their name at the top right.
  • Executive — enters and edits production, breakdowns, holds, plans, settings. No billing.
  • Die Shop — sees only Die Shop Jobs and updates their status.
  • View only — can look at everything, cannot change anything.
  • Admin — everything, including contractor billing and this page.
This is your single working system. Everything is saved in two files in your Google Drive — breakdown-data.json and wip-data.json. Please don't use the old Breakdown / WIP dashboards any more: they don't know the new data (quality holds, die concerns, billing, history) and could remove it. Safety rule: a file is only ever written if it loaded successfully first — a failed read can never blank out good data.
