Absence Hours by Operator

Feeds the Man category

Working hours = Mon/Tue/Wed/Thu/Sat 8:30 AM–6:00 PM (minus 3 breaks) · Fri 8:30 AM–12:00 PM. Off-shift and break time is excluded; OT counts only when ticked. Sunday is treated as a day off.
Absence converts to hours automatically — full day = 8h 25m, half day = 4h 13m, late/short leave = minutes entered — and rolls into that shop's Man category.
